About

Rendering and game engine engineering

coopa builds real-time rendering and game engine technology in C++20 and Vulkan: renderers, physics, water, procedural worlds, and the editors and tools that drive them. The work is developed on Linux and macOS and published as open source.

The flagship is toyengine, a 3D engine with a deferred PBR renderer and a Blender-style editor. It is built on a family of standalone libraries, including mapcoopa, which generates whole fantasy worlds from a seed.

Focus areas

Real-time rendering

Deferred PBR, shadows, screen-space effects, volumetrics and post-processing on Vulkan.

Simulation

Rigid bodies, XPBD cloth, Gerstner-wave water and buoyancy.

Procedural worlds

Seeded, deterministic generation of terrain, climate, rivers, roads and settlements.

Engine tools

Editors, asset pipelines, packaging and headless test harnesses.
