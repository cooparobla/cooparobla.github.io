class RingBuffer

Templates: typename T
Defined in: /Users/tblaney/Code/toyengine/libs/sfxcoopa/sfxcoopa/collections/ring_buffer.h

Lock-free SPSC circular buffer of T, capacity rounded up to a power of two.

Thread-safety contract:push()/write() may only be called by the single producer thread.pop()/read() may only be called by the single consumer thread.available_read()/available_write() are safe to call from either thread for best-effort/diagnostic observation (the true value may change concurrently).clear() is owner-thread-only, not thread-safe against a concurrent push/pop from the other side.

coopa::sfx::collections::RingBuffer<float>ring(4096); floatsamples[128]; uint32_twritten=ring.write(samples,128);//producerthread floatout[128]; uint32_tread=ring.read(out,128);//consumerthread
